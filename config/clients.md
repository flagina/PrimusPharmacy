## House rules for client work

- Nothing reaches a client without Alex. Bots draft; Alex sends.
- Client credentials live in the connector layer, never in this repo. A bot that finds a
  credential in a file stops and reports it rather than using it.
- A client's own analytics, ad accounts and Google Business Profile are Red — no bot touches
  them without a written instruction in this repo.
